Mental health is health.

One in five adults experiences a mental health condition each year, yet stigma and silence keep far too many people from asking for help. MindSpace was created to change that conversation.

We believe understanding is the first step toward healing. Through education, honest stories, and accessible resources, we want everyone — regardless of background or circumstance — to feel empowered to care for their mind as they would their body.

Know the warning signs

Recognizing these signs early — in yourself or someone you love — can make it easier to reach out before a crisis develops.

Persistent sadness

Feeling down, hopeless, or empty for more than two weeks, or losing interest in things once enjoyed.

Extreme mood changes

Sudden shifts between highs and lows, irritability, or feeling on edge without a clear cause.

Withdrawing from others

Pulling away from friends, family, work, or activities, and isolating for long periods.

Changes in sleep or appetite

Sleeping or eating much more or less than usual, or noticeable changes in energy levels.

If these signs sound familiar, please don’t wait — explore support options or talk to a trusted person today.

Everyday coping tools

Small, consistent habits can help protect and strengthen your mental well-being.

Move your body

Even a 10-minute walk can lower stress hormones and boost mood-supporting chemicals in the brain.

Protect your sleep

Aim for a consistent bedtime. Sleep is one of the strongest predictors of emotional resilience.

Talk it out

Share how you feel with a friend, family member, or therapist. Naming an emotion can lessen its weight.

Practice grounding

Try slow breathing or the 5-4-3-2-1 technique to calm your nervous system in the moment.

Journal your thoughts

Writing for even five minutes a day can help you process emotions and spot patterns over time.

Limit doomscrolling

Set boundaries with news and social media, especially before bed, to protect your mental energy.

If you or someone you know is struggling

Free, confidential support is available right now, day or night.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988 for free, confidential support, 24/7 (US).

Crisis Text Line

Text HOME to 741741 to reach a trained crisis counselor.

International Help

Visit findahelpline.com for crisis lines and support outside the US.

If you are in immediate danger, please call your local emergency number right away.
